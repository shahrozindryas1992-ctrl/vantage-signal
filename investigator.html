VANTAGE SIGNAL / PRODUCT CONCEPT

Protection that helps you see the risk—and decide what to do next.

Vantage Signal Investigator is our long-term vision for a connected digital protection workspace for individuals, organizations, and remote workers. The goal is to help people bring relevant signals together, understand possible exposure, and move from uncertainty toward informed action.

◈ CONCEPT IN DEVELOPMENT — NOT YET AVAILABLE

THE VISION

One workspace. Multiple layers of digital risk.

Digital protection is not one alert or one browser setting. A useful system could connect preventive controls, investigation, and clear guidance—while showing users what is observed, what is inferred, and what remains unknown.

01 / PREVENT

Help reduce risky actions

Work toward practical safeguards at the point where sensitive information is shared, pasted, uploaded, or used with AI tools.

  • Clear, contextual warnings
  • User-controlled review steps
  • Privacy-aware design
02 / INVESTIGATE

Make signals easier to understand

Explore a future workspace for organizing reports, events, evidence, and potential exposure into an understandable timeline.

  • Evidence and source tracking
  • Connected risk context
  • Visible confidence and limitations
03 / RESPOND

Turn findings into next steps

Help users understand practical actions after a concern is identified, with guidance suited to their role and situation.

  • Prioritized action checklists
  • Readable summaries
  • Human review for important decisions

A POSSIBLE WORKFLOW

From a signal to a clearer decision.

This is a product-design concept, not a description of currently available features.

STEP 01

Collect

Bring in information from explicitly supported sources and user-provided evidence.

STEP 02

Examine

Review patterns, context, timestamps, and possible relationships.

STEP 03

Validate

Separate confirmed facts from assumptions, alerts, and missing information.

STEP 04

Act

Present understandable options and document the next steps chosen.

BUILT IN STAGES

Complete protection is an ambition—not a claim we make today.

Vantage Guard is an early browser-extension prototype with limited pattern detection. Investigator is still a concept. A dependable protection platform would require careful engineering, security testing, privacy safeguards, supported integrations, and independent validation.

We will clearly distinguish prototype features, planned capabilities, research findings, and independently validated protections. No software can prevent every attack or identify every exposure.
