VANTAGE SIGNAL / INVESTIGATION 04

AI / CYBERSECURITY

When AI starts acting like an attacker.

As AI systems gain autonomy, the security question changes from what can an AI generate to what can an AI actually do.

An AI system with access, identity and tools is no longer just a chatbot. It becomes an actor inside a system.

The new attack surface

Agentic AI can plan tasks, call tools, interact with applications and operate across multiple steps. That creates useful capabilities — and a new class of security questions.

MODELgenerates a plan
IDENTITYgets permissions
TOOLStakes actions
DATAcreates consequences

Why permissions matter

The risk is not simply that a model produces a bad answer. It is that a compromised or misdirected agent could use legitimate permissions in unintended ways.

Security controls therefore need to cover identity, least privilege, tool access, monitoring, containment and human approval for consequential actions.

Attackers will adapt too

AI can lower the cost of reconnaissance, social engineering and other tasks. As the technology improves, defenders and attackers gain access to similar capabilities.

The practical question

Organizations adopting agents should ask: What is the most damaging action this system is technically capable of taking? Then reduce permissions and build controls around that answer.
