ABOUT VANTAGE SIGNAL

Technology is changing faster than trust.

Vantage Signal is a research and advisory platform focused on the human side of cybersecurity, AI, privacy and digital trust.

THE IDEA

Research you can understand. Risk you can act on.

Technology can be secure and systems can be well designed, but people still interact with those systems every day.

A shared password, an overlooked access permission, sensitive information entered into an AI tool, or a poorly handled employee transition can create risk that technology alone cannot solve.

Vantage Signal studies that human layer and turns complex issues into practical guidance for organizations and useful research for everyone.

  • AI workplace security
  • Human-driven cybersecurity risk
  • Privacy and data governance
  • Emerging threats and digital trust
