PRIVACY / IDENTITY

From face to profile: the expanding identity trail.

Facial recognition can turn a face into a starting point for connecting information that once lived in separate places.

A face is an identifier. The privacy risk grows when that identifier becomes a key that unlocks other information.

Identity enrichment

Facial recognition systems can compare an image against reference databases. Some systems and services go further by attempting to connect an identity to public or commercially available information.

FACEvisual identifier

The problem with connection

Information that feels harmless in isolation can become more sensitive when combined. A name, workplace, social account, location and historical record can form a much more detailed profile.

Why accuracy is not the only issue

False matches matter, but even accurate identification raises questions about consent, purpose, retention, access and whether people can challenge how their information was assembled.

The bigger signal

Modern identity systems are increasingly about enrichment rather than recognition alone. The key governance question is not simply “can the system identify someone?” It is what becomes possible after identification?
