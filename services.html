VANTAGE SIGNAL / PRODUCT LAB

Meet Vantage Guard.

A browser-extension prototype exploring how to help people notice potentially sensitive text before they paste it into a website or online tool.

THE PROBLEM

One paste can move information into the wrong place.

People regularly copy text between documents, websites, and AI tools. Sometimes that text includes credentials or personal information they did not intend to share.

Vantage Guard is being developed to explore a small, practical intervention at that moment: flag certain patterns, show the user what was detected, and let them decide whether to continue, redact, or cancel.

CURRENT PROTOTYPE

Small scope. Clear limits.

The current prototype checks pasted text locally in the browser against selected patterns. It is an early experiment, not comprehensive protection.

01
DETECT

Look for selected patterns.

Examples include some token-like strings, email addresses, US-style identifiers, and payment-card-like numbers.

02
REVIEW

Give the user a choice.

When a match is detected, the prototype can offer continue, redact, or cancel options.

03
LOCAL-FIRST

Designed for local handling.

The current detection flow runs in the browser; it does not demonstrate organization-wide monitoring or network inspection.

04
VALIDATE

Test before expanding.

Detection quality, accessibility, compatibility, and false positives/negatives need systematic testing.

Important limitation: Vantage Guard may miss sensitive information or flag harmless text. It does not inspect all activity, prevent every disclosure, or replace security controls, user judgment, or specialist advice.
